# LICENSING_AND_USAGE_NOTES

Status values used in the manifest (`license_status`):
- **CLEAR** — CC0, US federal government work, expired Crown copyright, or an open licence (CC BY / CC BY-SA / FAL / GFDL / OGL / GODL-India) whose terms are met by giving the credit in ATTRIBUTIONS.md (CC BY-SA also requires share-alike for adapted versions; GFDL requires the licence text — prefer to avoid GFDL items).
- **LIKELY CLEAR** — the source page states public domain because of age (e.g. Pakistani photographs more than 50 years old). Reasonable for a Pakistani school competition; confirm before wider publication.
- **VERIFY BEFORE PUBLIC USE** — provenance or licence is weak, conflicting, or the item is probably still in copyright. Kept in the archive and clearly marked; do not publish until cleared or replaced.

Authenticity values (`historical_authenticity`): ORIGINAL HISTORICAL PHOTOGRAPH · ORIGINAL DOCUMENT · NEWSPAPER · MAP · SATELLITE IMAGERY · DOCUMENTARY PHOTOGRAPH (modern events) · MODERN PHOTOGRAPH OF HISTORICAL SITE · COLORIZED HISTORICAL PHOTOGRAPH · STAND-IN (related subject, not the event) · RECONSTRUCTION — NOT AN ORIGINAL HISTORICAL PHOTOGRAPH · ARTWORK · TEXTURE · DATA VISUALIZATION.
No image in this archive was generated or altered to look historical. Two museum dioramas and one museum sculpture are included only because they are labelled RECONSTRUCTION/ARTWORK.
